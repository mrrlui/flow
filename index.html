Why Does This Happen? (The "Bucket" Math)

1. The Startup Penalty: Every time a light turns green, drivers take ~2 seconds to react and accelerate. After that, 1 car passes every ~2.2 seconds. So a 16-second green light only has 14 seconds of steady movement (~6.36 cars per cycle).

2. The All-Red Tax: Because 3 directions share one narrow 26m lane, every switch needs an All-Red pause (3 switches per cycle). With a short 52s cycle, 23.1% of the entire hour is spent with every light red.

3. Why Longer Cycles Help: Try clicking "Longer Cycle (90s) Efficiency" above. Even without changing the green-light ratios much, fewer light switches per hour means less time wasted on All-Red pauses and driver startup delays!
